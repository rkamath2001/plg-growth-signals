# Sharing rewards, Q4

From the growth team. Approved by finance for a $40,000 monthly budget.

We reward sharing that brings in people who actually use the product. A reward is earned when someone you
shared with completes their first agent action. Sending a link, or getting someone to sign up, earns nothing
on its own.

Who counts depends on where your company is:

- If your company does not yet have an enterprise agreement, shares to colleagues at your own company count.
  This is how teams find each other and how a company grows toward a real conversation with us.
- If your company already has an agreement, shares to your customers, partners and study participants outside
  the company count, as long as your workspace admin has turned on external sharing. Colleagues who already
  have a license never earn you a reward; they are already set up.

Shared agents and shared spaces show the product doing real work, so they earn more than a shared document.
Slack or email embeds and digests earn less. Referral links count like documents.

The reward is $20 in usage credits. Nobody can earn more than 10 rewards in a month.
